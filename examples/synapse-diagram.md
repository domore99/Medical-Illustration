# Example: Synaptic Transmission Diagram

**Audience:** Neuroscience students and biomedical educators.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Create an educational cross-section diagram of a generic chemical synapse.
Show a presynaptic terminal containing synaptic vesicles, a synaptic cleft,
postsynaptic membrane receptors, and a directional schematic of
neurotransmitter release and receptor interaction. Use concise English labels,
clear arrows, a white background, and a textbook-style illustration. Do not
invent specific receptor subtypes or physiological measurements.
```

## Review checklist

- Distinguish presynaptic from postsynaptic structures.
- Review arrow direction, vesicle placement, and label accuracy.
- Avoid depicting a simplified mechanism as exhaustive or universally applicable.
- Match all details to an authoritative neuroscience source.

**Tool:** https://medicalillustration.app/

Illustrations should be checked by a subject-matter expert before publication or instruction.
