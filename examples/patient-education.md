# Example: Patient Education — Knee Replacement Overview

**Audience:** Adult patients receiving a general explanation from their care team.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Create a calm, clear educational illustration showing a simplified before-
and-after overview of a total knee replacement. In the left panel, label the
femur, tibia, and knee joint. In the right panel, show a generic knee joint
with illustrative prosthetic femoral and tibial components. Use minimal,
plain-English labels and a reassuring but neutral visual style. Do not show
patient-specific anatomy, device brands, surgical dimensions, or guarantees
about outcomes.
```

## Review checklist

- Does the diagram clearly distinguish simplified education from personalized advice?
- Are bone and implant locations medically plausible?
- Is procedural or outcomes language absent unless supplied by a clinician?
- Have a qualified care professional check the graphic and accompanying text.

**Tool:** https://medicalillustration.app/tools/patient-education-illustration-generator

This is a communications draft, not informed consent or clinical guidance.
