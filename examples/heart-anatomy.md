# Example: Labeled Human Heart Anatomy

**Audience:** First-year medical students.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Illustrate a frontal cutaway of the human heart as a clear teaching diagram.
Label the four chambers, interventricular septum, aorta, pulmonary trunk,
superior and inferior vena cava, and major atrioventricular valves. Show the
relative orientation of major structures, with readable English leader lines
and a white background. Use an educational anatomical drawing style, without
patient-specific findings or invented measurements.
```

## Review checklist

- Confirm viewer left/right orientation versus anatomical right/left.
- Verify the chamber labels, valve positions, and major vessel attachments.
- Verify any color conventions and legends.
- Check the final figure against a trusted anatomy atlas.
